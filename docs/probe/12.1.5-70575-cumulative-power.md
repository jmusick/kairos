# Cumulative Power in combat: 12.1.5 (70575)

- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Data:** the three dummy sessions in `WoWCombatLog-103026_110311.txt` (11:03 to 11:38)
- **What it is:** the Midnight Season 2 Arcane 4-piece buff (aura `1296930`). The Sunfury APL casts Prismatic Bolt early only at `buff.cumulative_power.stack=8`.
- **Visibility:** secret in combat (`shouldBeSecret` true, secrecy 2), readable out of combat. No glow.

## Mechanics (from the combat log)

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| What builds it? | **Only Arcane Missiles**: +1 as the channel starts, then about +1 per tick | All 456 gains came during Missiles channels |
| How fast? | **A full channel reaches 8** | 29 of 31 channels from 0 ended at 8; the other two were a 7 and a cancelled channel |
| What removes it? | **At 8, the next Arcane Blast or Prismatic Bolt consumes it** | Every one of 57 removals happened at 8 stacks: 27 at a Bolt cast, 20 at a Blast, 10 with no cast nearby (expiry, or a re-apply as a queued Missiles starts) |
| How long does it last? | 12 s after the last gain or refresh | Matches the probe's 12 s duration; Missiles at 8 refreshes it |

## Estimator

`Kairos/Specs/MageArcane/CumulativePower.lua` models those rules from the player's own casts and Missiles channels:

- **Builds:** +1 at channel start plus 7 × the fraction of the channel that ran, capped at 8, rounded to whole stacks.
- **Consumed:** by Blast or Bolt at 8.
- **Expires:** 12 s after the last gain.
- **Re-anchored:** on out-of-combat reads.

## Results (replay, all three sessions, 372 decisions)

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Same answer for `= 8` | **365/372 (98%)** |
| Exact stacks | 360/372 |
| Picks changed by estimating it (Salvo held true) | 1/372 |

The first pass scored 77% on `= 8`, from two problems, both fixed:

- **Truncation:** a channel ended at its last tick has run about 97% of its length, so `1 + 7 × 0.97` floored to 7 (44 cases). Stacks arrive per tick, so the estimate now rounds.
- **Log lag in the scorer:** the log writes the consumption a few ms after the Blast or Bolt that caused it, so a decision taken right then saw a stale 8 (40 cases). The replay now applies the consumption at the cast, as it does for Barrage and Salvo.

The 7 remaining misses are clipped channels that still reached 8.

These rules come from the same logs they're scored on, but they're mechanics (what builds, what consumes, how long it lasts), not fitted constants.
