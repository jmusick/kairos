### Other observations

- **Aura lookups return `nil` for secret auras, not a secret value.** Blizzard's docs flag `GetAuraDataBySpellName` and `GetPlayerAuraBySpellID` as `RequiresNonSecretAura`. A `nil` in combat means "hidden", not "absent". The state model must check `C_Secrets.ShouldSpellAuraBeSecret` before treating `nil` as "aura down".
- **Prismatic Bolt (`1295924`) overrides Arcane Blast** on the action bar once it's active (slot 1 changed from 30451). Keybind lookup already handles overrides.
- Arcane Soul and Lustrous Gleam never appeared, so their IDs are still unknown. They may not be talented or may not have triggered.

### Runs #4 and #5 (seconds after fighting)

- **The Arcane Barrage glow tracks Salvo at 25.** Glow on at 25 stacks (#3, #5), off at 24 (#4), and on during combat in #2. Three out of three so far. If it holds, the glow answers `buff.arcane_salvo.stack=25` in combat for free.
- **Clearcasting shows through Arcane Missiles.** Missiles was usable and glowing whenever Clearcasting was up (1 stack in #4, 2 in #5), and neither when it was down (#1 to #3). That covers the Sunfury branch, which only needs Clearcasting presence. Clearcasting lasts 30 s and stacks to at least 2.
- **Prismatic Bolt is a proc that replaces Arcane Blast.** While it's up, Prismatic Bolt glows and action slot 1 reads `1295924`. When it's gone (#5), the glow is off and the slot reads Arcane Blast again.
- **The Season 2 4-piece is live.** Cumulative Power `1296930` sat at 8 stacks with a 12 s duration, so the APL's `buff.cumulative_power.stack=8` line applies to this character.
- **Arcane Surge's buff lasts 17.4 s.** Cast at 486082.36, buff expired at 486099.76. Touch of the Magi went out 2 s after Surge.
- **Haste moves during the burst window:** 30.5% with Surge up (#4) against 27.3% base. An out-of-combat haste snapshot will run low during burst.
- **Nameplates do report combat.** A nameplate read `affectingCombat = true` in #5, so the earlier `false` reflects how dummies behave. Still confirm on real mobs.
- **Salvo moves during a pull:** 25 before, 24 in #4, back to 25 by #5.

## What's next for Salvo

The Sunfury branch compares Salvo stacks against 8, 12 and 25, so they have to be modeled in combat. Two things decide whether that's possible:

1. **Do aura events still identify Salvo in combat?** The fight trace records every in-combat `UNIT_AURA` update to Salvo's aura instance (learned out of combat). If those arrive with readable IDs, each one marks a stack change even though the count is hidden.
2. **How does Salvo build?** Kairos now turns on advanced combat logging itself. The log records every Salvo stack change (`SPELL_AURA_APPLIED_DOSE`) next to the cast that caused it. `tools/kairos-dump.lua combatlog` extracts exactly that.

Both now collect without any commands: play a few pulls, `/reload` or log out, and the data is in SavedVariables and `_retail_\Logs`.

## Probe changes made after these runs

- Aura rows now include `bySpellID`, `instanceID`, `byInstance` (`GetAuraDataByAuraInstanceID`) and `displayCount` (`GetAuraApplicationDisplayCount`).
- New `auraEvents` rows count in-combat `UNIT_AURA` payloads: total, secret parts, added auras with secret spell IDs, and updates or removals per known aura instance.
- New rows: `GetHaste`, `IsPlayerMoving`, movement events since combat start, and threat on the target and a nameplate.
- `State:Moving()` falls back to `PLAYER_STARTED_MOVING` and `PLAYER_STOPPED_MOVING`, marked estimated until the probe shows they fire in combat.
