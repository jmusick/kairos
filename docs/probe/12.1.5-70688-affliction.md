## What went wrong in the fight

Kairos counted **11 enemies**: every hostile nameplate in the Silvermoon training area, whether or not it was in combat. That routed the Hellcaller APL to `HC_aoe`, which recommended Seed of Corruption at every shard on a single dummy; the player followed it 14 times. Secret Shard Instability then forced Assisted Combat after each spend, and Cascading Calamity was unknown.

Fixes in the same change:

- Affliction and Destruction count only hostile nameplates **in combat** (D24), clamped to at least 1. Re-evaluating the 49 recorded states with one enemy gives the single-target list throughout: Haunt, Agony, Wither, Malevolence, Unstable Affliction and Malefic Grasp, and no Seed. On a dummy pack this reads as single target too, because dummies never report combat.
- Shard Instability's presence comes from the UA/Seed glow when its aura is secret.
- Cascading Calamity is timed from own casts: 15 s from a UA that lands while our UA is still on the target (SimC's trigger). With Fatal Echoes, which can grant it at random, a modeled zero is unknown.

There is no Affliction log replay yet, so these picks are checked against the APL on Kairos's own recorded state, not against log truth.
