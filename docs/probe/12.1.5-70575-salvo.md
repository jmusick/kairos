# Arcane Salvo in combat: 12.1.5 (70575)

- **Date:** 2026-10-30, about 50 s of casting on the Falconwing Square dummies (11:03:11 to 11:04:05)
- **Data:** Kairos fight traces and probes (`SavedVariables\Kairos.lua`) plus the advanced combat log Kairos turned on (`Logs\WoWCombatLog-103026_110311.txt`)
- **Method:** the trace's 37 recorded casts were aligned to the combat log's casts (37 out of 37 matched within 0.15 s). Every trace event was then compared with the true aura state from the log.

This answers the gate question in SPEC §10.2 for the Sunfury prototype: can Kairos know Arcane Salvo stacks in combat?

## Findings

| Question | Answer | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Can the stack count be read in combat? | **No.** Aura lookups return `nil` (the aura is `RequiresNonSecretAura`) | Probes #2 and later |
| Do in-combat `UNIT_AURA` events identify Salvo? | **No.** Every in-combat payload had secret parts, and none matched Salvo's instance ID | 11 of 11 and 23 of 23 events secret in the two fights that had events |
| Is `stack=25` visible in combat? | **Yes, through the Arcane Barrage proc glow** | The glow turned on within 5 ms of Salvo reaching 25 every time (5 out of 5) |
| Does anything else light Barrage? | **Arcane Soul (`451038`) and Intuition (`1223797`)** | All 13 glow changes are explained by Salvo at 25, Arcane Soul or Intuition |
| Are out-of-combat reads accurate? | **Yes** | The trace's reads match the log, for example Salvo 15 at 11:03:49.99, 0.12 s before Barrage |

The one glow change flagged as a mismatch (11:03:25.178) is an on and off within the same millisecond as a Barrage cast, so the log still shows the pre-cast stacks.

### How Salvo builds

| Source | Salvo gained per cast (this log) |
|---|---|
| Arcane Missiles channel | 8, 9, 11, 12, 12, 13, 13, 14 (and 0 for one clipped channel) |
| Arcane Missiles with Overpowered Missiles | 8, 9, 11, 15, 20, 20 |
| Prismatic Bolt | 6, 6 |
| Arcane Blast | +2 each, +3 with a Meteorite hit (10 straight Blasts from 11:15:39 to 11:16:03). An earlier "1, 3, 3" came from misattributed events |
| Arcane Barrage | Consumes all stacks (the aura is removed) |
| Arcane Barrage during Arcane Soul | **Adds about +5 instead** (1 → 7 → 12 → 18 across three Soul Barrages; the first Barrage after Soul consumed as usual) |

- Missiles adds about +1 per missile tick, with extra +1s from Meteorite hits (random procs). With Overpowered Missiles, ticks add up to +3 each.
- Salvo has no duration: it persists between fights, so the pre-pull value is always known.
- Arcane Soul starts the instant Arcane Surge's buff ends (11:03:35.947) and lasts about 4.1 s. During it Barrage glows and gets cast repeatedly.

## What this means for the gate

- **`buff.arcane_salvo.stack=25`** (and `>=25`) is observable in combat: Barrage glowing while Arcane Soul and Intuition are both down. Kairos can time Arcane Soul from its own Arcane Surge cast. Intuition's trigger isn't known yet; at 4 of the 5 moments Salvo hit 25, Intuition was up too.
- **Thresholds 8 and 12** can't be read. They have to be estimated from casts: reset on Barrage, +3 per Arcane Blast, +6 per Prismatic Bolt, about +12 per Missiles channel. The spread per Missiles channel (8 to 14, more with Overpowered Missiles) puts both thresholds inside the estimate's error band right after one Missiles channel.
- Whether that error costs enough top-icon agreement to trip the gate (SPEC §9, ≥ 90%) is the next measurement. It needs the Sunfury APL's actual Salvo conditions and a replay that runs the estimator against the log's true stacks.

## Deducing Salvo in combat (Sunfury)

The Sunfury list (SimC `midnight` at `515f4a3fe9`, 2026-09-06) reads Salvo in four places:

| Line | Salvo condition | Observable in combat? |
|---|---|---|
| `arcane_missiles,if=buff.clearcasting.react&buff.arcane_salvo.stack<12` (top line) | `< 12` | Estimated |
| `arcane_barrage,if=buff.arcane_charge.stack=4&(…buff.clearcasting.react…)&buff.arcane_salvo.react>=12` | `>= 12` | Estimated. Charges and Clearcasting (Missiles glow) are readable |
| `…\|((buff.arcane_surge.remains>gcd.max\|buff.arcane_surge.down)&buff.arcane_salvo.react=25)` | `= 25` | **Yes:** Barrage glow with Arcane Soul and Intuition down |
| `…\|buff.arcane_soul.up\|(buff.arcane_salvo.react>8&cooldown.touch_of_the_magi.ready)` | `> 8` | Estimated |

So in practice there's one hidden decision: with Clearcasting up and 4 charges, Missiles (Salvo < 12) or Barrage (Salvo ≥ 12).

The estimate never drifts far, because it's re-anchored every cycle:

- **Exactly 0** after every Barrage (the aura is removed).
- **Exactly 25** when Barrage lights up outside Arcane Soul and Intuition, and **at most 24** while it's dark.
- Between anchors, add per cast: about +3 for Arcane Blast, +6 for Prismatic Bolt, about +12 for a Missiles channel, and about +20 with Overpowered Missiles (which usually reaches 25, where the glow takes over).

The one random part is the Missiles channel. Its 9 to 15 gain isn't explained by anything Kairos could count: missile hits (8 to 12) and Meteorite hits (0 to 7) don't predict it. Per channel, from this log:

| Before | After | Gain | Missile hits | Meteorites | Overpowered Missiles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 14 | 13 | 9 | 6 | no |
| 3 | 17 | 14 | 9 | 1 | no |
| 2 | 17 | 15 | 10 | 5 | no |
| 6 | 15 | 9 | 10 | 0 | no |
| 2 | 11 | 9 | 8 | 7 | no |
| 6 | 19 | 13 | 9 | 0 | no |
| 1 | 13 | 12 | 12 | 5 | no |
| 2 | 13 | 11 | 11 | 4 | no |
| 5 | 25 | 20 | 9 | 5 | yes |
| 5 | 25 | 20 | 8 | 4 | yes |

**Overpowered Missiles doesn't need to be detected.** The Arcane Missiles glow tracks Clearcasting (15 of 16 glow changes; the other is a same-millisecond spend and re-proc), so it can't show Overpowered Missiles, and that aura is secret in combat. It's a random proc: it was applied at Salvo 24, 12, 15 and 23, mid-channel or alongside Clearcasting and Brainstorm on a Blast. It empowers the next Missiles channel, and all three empowered channels in this log ended at 25 (+20, capped from 17, +20), where the Barrage glow reports the stacks exactly. Assuming a normal channel when it was empowered only underestimates, and both cases land on the ≥ 12 side, so it never flips the Missiles-or-Barrage decision.

After one Missiles channel from a low start, Salvo was **≥ 12 in 7 of 8 cases**. The miss ended at 11, one stack short, where Barrage and one more Missiles are close in value. An estimator that adds +12 per channel would have picked the same side of 12 as the true stacks in about 7 of 8 decisions here.

## Estimator v1, replayed against the log

`Kairos/Specs/MageArcane/Salvo.lua` implements the model above. `tools/kairos-dump.lua salvo <log> <player>` replays a combat log through that same file and scores the estimate at every cast against the log's true stacks:

- The Barrage glow is synthesized from the log: lit while true Salvo is 25 or Arcane Soul is up, which the trace confirmed for 13 of 13 glow changes.
- True stacks are given to the estimator only at the start and after 6 s gaps, standing in for the out-of-combat reads it gets in game. Everything else comes from casts.

On the whole log (11:03 to 11:16, 61 casts with Salvo known):

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Same side of 12 (the Missiles-or-Barrage decision) | **60 / 61 (98%)** |
| Same side of 12, true stacks 6 to 18 | 21 / 22 (95%) |
| Same side of 12, at Missiles and Barrage casts | 36 / 37 (97%) |
| Same side of 9 (`> 8`, with Touch ready) | 60 / 61 (98%) |
| Same answer for `= 25` | 61 / 61 (100%) |
| Mean absolute error | 0.93 stacks (max 5) |
| True stacks inside the low–high bounds | 49 / 61 (80%) |

The one miss is a Missiles channel that gained 11 against the assumed 12, the randomness no observable explains.

**Caveat: this is in-sample.** The first replay (89%) exposed three things that were then fixed: Blast is +2 not +3, Barrage adds instead of consuming during Arcane Soul, and the scorer must treat Barrage as consuming at the cast. The 98% is measured on the same log the numbers came from. The next session, especially a dungeon, is the real test. Since Kairos turns on combat logging itself, scoring it only takes `tools/kairos-dump.lua salvo` on the new log.

### Out-of-sample check (second session, 11:23:55 to 11:27:26)

One sustained 182 s fight on the dummy, 124 casts with Salvo known, scored with `salvo <log> Merlinmonroe 11:23:55`.

| Measure | v1 as built (out of sample) | v2 after fixes | v2 on session 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same side of 12 | **111/124 (90%)** | 116/124 (94%) | 57/61 (93%) |
| … at Missiles and Barrage casts | 72/79 (91%) | 75/79 (95%) | 34/37 (92%) |
| Same side of 9 | 122/124 (98%) | 123/124 (99%) | 60/61 (98%) |
| Same answer for `= 25` | 123/124 (99%) | 123/124 (99%) | 61/61 (100%) |
| Mean / max error | 1.44 / 24 | 0.99 / 5 | 1.07 / 6 |

The 90% is the honest out-of-sample result for v1. v2 changes, made after seeing this session:

- **Bug: Arcane Soul could stay "on" forever.** When Salvo reached 25 during Soul, the glow stayed lit when Soul ended, so no glow-off arrived and the next real Barrage was treated as a Soul Barrage (estimate 24, truth 0 to 4, twice). Soul is now timed purely from the Surge cast: it started 17.32, 17.43 and 17.45 s after the cast and lasted 4.09, 3.96 and 3.98 s. If the glow is still lit when Soul ends, Salvo is 25.
- **Missiles is +11, not +12.** Uncapped, non-empowered channels gained 8 to 13 this session (mean about 10.8). Pooled with session 1 it's about 11. All 9 empowered channels reached 25.

v2 lands at 93 to 94% on the 12 line in both sessions. Its remaining misses sit within 1 to 3 stacks of 12, the channel-to-channel randomness Kairos can't observe. The next new session is v2's out-of-sample test.

**The in-game estimator matches the replay.** During session 2 it recorded 24 channel stops, 22 glow anchors, 78 cast updates and 1 read. Aligned to the log (126 of 126 casts), its live estimates were on the right side of 12 for 109 of 126 casts (87%), against 90% for the same v1 model in the replay. The small gap is expected, since the recorder logs at channel stop while the replay credits partial channels.

### Third session: v2 out of sample (11:32:48 to 11:37:44)

A 260 s fight plus three short ones, 181 casts with Salvo known, scored with `salvo <log> Merlinmonroe 11:32:45`.

| Measure | v2, out of sample |
|---|---|
| Same side of 12 | **163/181 (90%)** |
| … at Missiles and Barrage casts | 108/120 (90%) |
| Same side of 9 | 176/181 (97%) |
| Same answer for `= 25` | 180/181 (99%) |
| Mean / max error | 1.22 / 7 |
| In-game estimates vs truth (183 casts) | 163/183 (89%), mean error 0.79, max 5 |

All 18 misses are underestimates (the model said 10 or 11, the truth was 12 to 17), the mirror image of session 2's overestimates.

**The limit is the Missiles roll, not the calibration.** v1 and v2 both scored 90% on data they hadn't seen. The uncapped, non-empowered channel gain is 8 to 16, mean 11.1 to 11.3 in every session, and the same with 2 or 5 enemies hit (77 channels across all sessions). After one channel from a low start, the true value sits on either side of 12 about evenly, so no fixed gain can do better on those decisions. **Retuning the mean further would only fit noise; don't.** Getting past about 90% on the 12 line would need a new observable, and none is known.

**In game equals the replay.** With the comparison corrected to count a channel-stop estimate stamped at the same instant as the next cast (chained Missiles), the live v2 estimator scored 89% against the replay's 90%. All 182 recorded casts matched the log within 0.15 s.

**What this means for the gate:** about 1 in 10 Missiles-or-Barrage decisions will be off, always within a few stacks of 12. How much that costs overall top-icon agreement (SPEC §9 target ≥ 90%) depends on how often those decisions decide the top pick. That's what the Sunfury APL replay (Assisted Combat vs the APL on true state vs Kairos) will measure.

### Fourth session (12:35:52 to 12:40:51)

One sustained 291 s fight, 192 decisions, with the Cumulative Power estimator running alongside.

| Measure | Replay | Live in game |
|---|---|---|
| Same side of 12 | 178/192 (93%) | 180/194 (93%) |
| Same side of 9 / same `= 25` | 98% / 99% | |
| Mean / max error | 1.07 / 5 | 0.59 / 11 |

**The live 11-stack miss exposed a bug the replay can't show: chained Missiles.** When Missiles is recast mid-channel (the APL's `chain=1`), the game fires the new channel's `UNIT_SPELLCAST_CHANNEL_START` before its cast event. `OnChannelStart` then gave the still-open first channel the second channel's end time, so it looked twice as long and was credited about half its stacks (6 instead of 11). The replay drives channels from the log's casts and ticks, so it never hit this. Fixed in both estimators: a channel start well after the open channel's start closes it first, and the cast event right after doesn't reopen it.

The bounds are too narrow (80% coverage). That comes partly from Meteorite hits and partly from the log not giving a stack count on `SPELL_AURA_APPLIED`, which the replay reads as 1. The point estimate is what decisions use, so this matters only for future confidence styling.

## Collection issues found and fixed

- **Auto-probe never ran.** Saved settings kept the old `autoProbe = false` default. A schema migration now turns it on once.
- **Fight markers raced the dummy.** Open-world dummies drop combat within seconds of a pause, often mid-channel. The end-of-fight marker was written 0.3 s late and could land inside the next fight. The end marker is now immediate, and the after-fight aura read is its own `after` entry, skipped if combat restarts first.
- `tools/kairos-dump.lua events` prints the raw recorder stream, including events between fights.
- Arcane Soul and Intuition are now tracked auras with known IDs.
