# Demonology live review: 12.1.5 (70688), Nov. 1, 2026

The first three live Demonology sessions: a dummy pack and two Timewalking
dungeons. Kairos agreed with the APL clearly more often than Assisted Combat in
all three. Grading the icon on screen at each press against the APL rebuilt from
the combat log:

| Session | Build | Kairos in game | Assisted Combat | Your casts |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dummy pack (2.5 min) | 0.2.1+ | **91/103 (88%)** | 77/103 (75%) | 96/109 (88%) |
| The Everbloom (10 min) | 0.2.1+ | **177/222 (80%)** | 145/225 (64%) | 194/246 (79%) |
| Shadowmoon Burial Grounds (10 min) | 0.2.2 | **150/170 (88%)** | 97/170 (57%) | 153/195 (78%) |

Implosion accounts for most misses: 29 of 45 in The Everbloom and 14 of 20 in
Shadowmoon Burial Grounds. Outside it, Kairos agrees on 164/180 (91%) and
137/143 (96%). The cause is the imp estimate (see below), which has improved
since each session ran. The sessions found five live-model errors, now fixed,
and one recorder sampling flaw, which also affects Arcane.

## What the client exposes in combat

- Soul Shards stay readable (secrecy 0).
- The Demonic Core aura is secret in combat even in the open world, so the
  adapter uses Demonbolt's glow for presence only. A hidden Core count never
  changed a pick (0 of 355). For this build, only Power Siphon reads the count,
  and it isn't talented.
- After Grimoire: Imp Lord is cast, its spellbook entry resolves to 132411.
- **Implosion's cast count (`C_Spell.GetSpellCastCount`) is the Wild Imp count.**
  In Shadowmoon Burial Grounds it was readable in 57 of 61 out-of-combat watch
  samples and secret in 16 of 18 in-combat ones. Where readable, the log's imp
  count matched it exactly only 27/59 times and was usually low: the log can't
  see imps sitting idle.

## Reference validity

The log never states shards: 12.1.5 cast events carry only mana. The replay
rebuilds shards from energize events and costs. The logged amount is the
effective gain and the next field is what the cap wasted (a capped Demonbolt
logs "1.8, 0.2"); the maximum is logged as 50. At each client combat start, it
reseeds from the client's last traced reading, because shards regenerate
between pulls. The rounded-down model then matches 84/87, 188/201 and 155/172
later client readings within 0.15 s.

Corrections the reference needed:

- An instant Demonbolt's +2 energize (ID 280127) is logged about 1 ms before
  its own CAST_SUCCESS. It is an effect of the press, so it is excluded from
  that press's state (26 and 86 presses).
- Costs from the client's readings: Hand of Gul'dan 3, Imp Lord 1, Tyrant 0,
  Ruination 0, Call Dreadstalkers 0.
- An imp's life ends at its own last action, not when its final Firebolt lands
  about a second later.

## Live-model errors found and fixed

| Error | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Imp Lord stayed recommended after casting | The resync that runs out of combat read the replaced spell as idle, in the same instant as the cast | An idle read can't cancel the cast's own cooldown within 2 s or while the spell is replaced |
| Dreadstalkers required and spent 2 shards | 7/7 casts free in client readings | No shard requirement or cost; APL regenerated |
| Imps expired after 5 Firebolts | Dummy: 59/87 Hand imps cast 6. Dungeon: 131/210 cast 6. None cast more | Lifetime is 6 Firebolts (12 s ÷ haste) |
| Implosion left no imps | 8/8 and 20/20 Implosions summoned 3 imps | With To Hell and Back, Implosion spawns 3 (talent link inferred) |
| Imps expired between pulls | Dungeon imps stopped casting for exactly the 18 s the player was out of combat, then resumed | The lifetime clock pauses out of combat, keeps whole casts, and resumes 3 s after combat starts |
| Imps the estimate couldn't see | Inner Demons and idle carried imps | Kairos anchors to Implosion's cast count wherever it's readable (between pulls) |

## Wild Imps: the remaining gap

Imps spend energy only on casts, and only with a target. Between pulls they keep
their remaining whole Firebolts; carried imps cast again a median 2.1–2.3 s after
combat starts (about 0.7 s to re-engage plus one cast). The estimate pauses out
of combat, keeps whole casts, and resumes 3 s after combat starts. The 3 s was
chosen across all three sessions, so it also absorbs target gaps within combat:
it is fitted, not validated. Between pulls, the estimate snaps to Implosion's
readable cast count.

With every change, the replay's imp estimate gives the same APL pick as log
imps at 109/109, 234/246 and 188/195 decisions, and at 32/34 Implosion
decisions in Shadowmoon Burial Grounds, where Kairos in game got 13/27.
Replaying the traced counts (the live adapter reads them more often) put the
estimate within one imp at 77/196 Shadowmoon decisions, up from 52.

Still unseen live: imps idling without a target in combat (six-cast imps'
90th-percentile life was 16.2 s against a modeled 9.3 s), and Inner Demons' imp
every 12.0 s, whose timer runs out of combat and can shift. The log reference
also undercounts idle imps, so imp-heavy Implosion calls are its least certain
rows.

## Sampling: grade what was on screen at the press

The client starts the GCD at the keypress, and the 0.1 s display refresh often
evaluates the next GCD before UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT, START or SUCCEEDED arrive.
Snapshots taken at those events had already advanced past the press:

- Dummy: 79 of 103 START/success snapshots.
- Dungeon: 119 of 225 snapshots, even at SENT.

For these sessions, the scorer replaces each such snapshot with the display
change shown just before that GCD began (its projected end minus one GCD).
Without that, the dummy scored 86/103 and the dungeon 168/224.

Going forward, the recorder keeps the last 20 evaluations by reference. At each
commit it reads the GCD start and saves the latest evaluation from before it,
with the later pick kept as `forecast`. Arcane's commitments get the same
correction. Arcane's scorer doesn't yet use SENT snapshots or the pick-stream
fallback.

## Remaining misses outside Implosion

16 in The Everbloom and 6 in Shadowmoon Burial Grounds: mostly shard or proc
timing at the press, plus Dreadstalkers coming ready within the projected GCD.
In Shadowmoon, every snapshot was taken before the press's GCD, so the 0.2.2
recorder fix works live, and all 170 captured evaluations reproduce exactly.
