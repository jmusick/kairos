## Reference validity

The log never states shards: 12.1.5 cast events carry only mana. The replay
rebuilds shards from energize events and costs. The logged amount is the
effective gain and the next field is what the cap wasted (a capped Demonbolt
logs "1.8, 0.2"); the maximum is logged as 50. At each client combat start, it
reseeds from the client's last traced reading, because shards regenerate
between pulls. The rounded-down model then matches 84/87, 188/201, 155/172,
143/152 and 117/131 later client readings within 0.15 s.

Corrections the reference needed:

- An instant Demonbolt's +2 energize (ID 280127) is logged about 1 ms before
  its own CAST_SUCCESS. It is an effect of the press, so it is excluded from
  that press's state (26 and 86 presses).
- Costs from the client's readings: Hand of Gul'dan 3, Imp Lord 1, Tyrant 0,
  Ruination 0, Call Dreadstalkers 0.
- An imp's life ends at its own last action, not when its final Firebolt lands
  about a second later.

## Live-model errors found and fixed

| Error | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Imp Lord stayed recommended after casting | The resync that runs out of combat read the replaced spell as idle, in the same instant as the cast | An idle read can't cancel the cast's own cooldown within 2 s or while the spell is replaced |
| Dreadstalkers required and spent 2 shards | 7/7 casts free in client readings | No shard requirement or cost; APL regenerated |
| Imps expired after 5 Firebolts | Dummy: 59/87 Hand imps cast 6. Dungeon: 131/210 cast 6. None cast more | Lifetime is 6 Firebolts (12 s ÷ haste) |
| Implosion left no imps | 8/8 and 20/20 Implosions summoned 3 imps | With To Hell and Back, Implosion spawns 3 (talent link inferred) |
| Imps expired between pulls | Dungeon imps stopped casting for exactly the 18 s the player was out of combat, then resumed | The lifetime clock pauses out of combat, keeps whole casts, and resumes 3 s after combat starts |
| Imps the estimate couldn't see | Inner Demons and idle carried imps | Kairos anchors to Implosion's cast count wherever it's readable (between pulls) |

## Wild Imps: the remaining gap

Imps spend energy only on casts, and only with a target. Between pulls they keep
their remaining whole Firebolts; carried imps cast again a median 2.1–2.3 s after
combat starts (about 0.7 s to re-engage plus one cast). The estimate pauses out
of combat, keeps whole casts, and resumes 3 s after combat starts. The 3 s was
chosen across all three sessions, so it also absorbs target gaps within combat:
it is fitted, not validated. Between pulls, the estimate snaps to Implosion's
readable cast count.

With every change, the replay's imp estimate gives the same APL pick as log
imps at 109/109, 234/246 and 188/195 decisions, and at 32/34 Implosion
decisions in Shadowmoon Burial Grounds, where Kairos in game got 13/27.
Replaying the traced counts (the live adapter reads them more often) put the
estimate within one imp at 77/196 Shadowmoon decisions, up from 52.

Grimrail Depot was the first fresh log for these rules. The anchor worked live:
at 5 of 7 pull starts the captured estimate equalled the last readable Implosion
count, and the other two were within two. But Implosion was still 8/23 in game,
and the replayed estimate gave the true-state pick at only 119/134 decisions.
The cause is imps idling inside combat. During the Nitrogg fight they stopped
casting for 17 s (12:02:14.8–12:02:31.3) while the player cast only Burning
Rush, so 14 imps lived on while the estimate read 0–3.

Pausing the estimate whenever the player hasn't cast for 4 s helps Grimrail
(119 → 130/134) but hurts Shadowmoon (188 → 184/195), and it would be tuned on
its own validation log, so it isn't used. Imps attack the player's target, so
the recorder now logs whether a live hostile target exists (on change, on
target health events, and with every commit).

Bloodmaul Slag Mines was the first session with target logging. The target
read was never secret in combat. The player was in combat without a live
hostile target in 27 spans, about 45 s in all. Most were target swaps under a
second; the longest were 7.6 s as Magmolatus died and 9.2 s at the start of
Gug'rokk. The imps stopped casting in both: after Magmolatus from 12:55:16, and
at Gug'rokk from 12:57:39 until 12:57:52, 3 s after the target came back.

So the estimate now treats a lost target like leaving combat: it pauses, keeps
whole casts, and resumes `REENGAGE` (3 s) after a target returns. On Bloodmaul
the imp estimate's pick matched the true-state pick at 197/200 decisions, up
from 185, and at 28/31 Implosion decisions, up from 16. Resuming after 0, 1 or
2 s scored 194–195, so the existing 3 s stays and the rule adds no constant.
The other four sessions have no target entries and are unchanged.

Skyreach, played on the new build, is the fresh check. It had less targetless
combat: 21 spans, about 28 s. The two longest behaved as modeled. In 9.5 s
without a target (13:14:03–13:14:13) the imps cast nothing until 13:14:16, 3 s
after the target came back, and in a 4.2 s gap they stopped at 13:14:52, as the
target was lost. Replayed, the pause moved little here: the imp estimate's pick
matched at 150/152 decisions either way, and at 19/20 Implosion decisions
against 18/20 without it. In game Implosion was 16/19, the best so far, but the
estimate scores nearly as well without the pause, so most of that gain comes
from this run rather than the rule. The mechanism held on a fresh log, with no
regression; the size of its gain rests on Bloodmaul alone.

Still unseen live: Inner Demons' imp every 12.0 s, whose timer runs out of
combat and can shift. The log reference
also undercounts idle imps, so imp-heavy Implosion calls are its least certain
rows.

## Sampling: grade what was on screen at the press

The client starts the GCD at the keypress, and the 0.1 s display refresh often
evaluates the next GCD before UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT, START or SUCCEEDED arrive.
Snapshots taken at those events had already advanced past the press:

- Dummy: 79 of 103 START/success snapshots.
- Dungeon: 119 of 225 snapshots, even at SENT.

For these sessions, the scorer replaces each such snapshot with the display
change shown just before that GCD began (its projected end minus one GCD).
Without that, the dummy scored 86/103 and the dungeon 168/224.

Going forward, the recorder keeps the last 20 evaluations by reference. At each
commit it reads the GCD start and saves the latest evaluation from before it,
with the later pick kept as `forecast`. Arcane's commitments get the same
correction. Arcane's scorer doesn't yet use SENT snapshots or the pick-stream
fallback.

## Dominion of Argus: fixed duration, no refresh

The evening Everbloom run (18:11–18:21, four bosses, 0.2.6, with a new point in
Dominion of Argus's second rank) had 27 misses. Thirteen were Hand of Gul'dan
picked by `hand_of_guldan,if=buff.dominion_of_argus.up` after the buff had
ended. The adapter set Argus to 15 s at Tyrant and pushed it to 15 s past every
Hand or Ruination cast inside it, so steady Hands kept it up until the next
Tyrant.

The log shows neither part. Every window starts at Tyrant and ends exactly
15.0 s later at rank 1 (33 windows across the earlier logs) or 20.0 s later at
rank 2 (5 windows tonight). Hands only flip its stack between 1 and 2 (every
second Hand summons a demon). The talent tooltip agrees: one four-rank node
(110404). Ranks 2–3 add 5 s each, and rank 4 refunds a shard per Hand. Out of
combat the aura is readable and reported `duration` 20.

The adapter now runs Argus from Tyrant for 15 s + 5 s per extra rank, read from
`C_Traits` out of combat, or from the aura when readable. Rescoring each
captured decision with Argus timed that way (the log's Tyrant times, everything

## 0.2.7 live: Argus holds, and the shard reading lags casts

Skyreach (18:45–18:53) and The Blinding Vale (18:53–19:05, a Midnight dungeon
on normal) ran 0.2.7. No miss in either came from Dominion of Argus, and
Implosion was 36/36 in the Vale.

Seven of Skyreach's first-pass misses (18:46:18–18:46:43) were the
reference's. The log has no Demonic Core until 18:46:29 and no Argus until the
18:46:44 Tyrant, but the replay still held both from the Everbloom run 25
minutes earlier. Auras that expire during a loading screen are never logged as
removed. The replay now clears its log auras at each ZONE_CHANGE, which moved
Skyreach from 140 to 148/154 and Bloodmaul from 150 to 152/173.

The largest live error was shard timing. A finished cast's shards reach
`UnitPower` after UNIT_SPELLCAST_SUCCEEDED (0–0.3 s later in the trace). Kairos
stops projecting the cast when it ends, so for a refresh or two it evaluated
the old count. After a Shadow Bolt, a pick was drawn in that gap in 63 of 95
casts and differed from the next pick in 34; after an Infernal Bolt, 15 of 18.
Hand of Gul'dan's cost always arrived with its cast event, and an instant
Demonbolt's gain is logged before its success. With spell queueing, the next
press often lands in exactly that gap. At 18:56:43 an Infernal Bolt ended
130 ms before a Hand press, and the snapshot showed Demonbolt on 2 shards
instead of 5.

The adapter now holds a projected cast's gain after its success until the
reading leaves its value during the cast, for at most `POWER_LAG` (0.5 s).

## Night session: eleven dungeons, three recorded

The night session ran eleven Timewalking dungeons (19:06–21:12) on 0.2.7 plus
the cast shard hold. Only the last three left recorder data. The pull archive
keeps 32 combat segments (about 126 KB each, 4 MB in all), which covered the
last 30 minutes. The flight ring had rolled over too, and with it the shard
readings that seed the reference, so the first eight dungeons can't be scored.
The session had 257 combat segments. After this review the default rose to
300 segments, each segment also keeps the shard readings, fight edges, target
changes and watches, and a press saves its snapshot once instead of up to three
times (999 of 1,896 snapshots repeated the one before). That roughly halves a
segment, to an estimated 100 KB with the added traces, so an evening like this
one saves on the order of 30 MB.

Kairos missed 13 of 473 graded presses in the three recorded runs:

- Three Demonbolt calls where a Core pair arrived 60–100 ms before the press.
  Pairs come from Dreadstalkers 12.5–13.3 s after the cast (200 pairs
  tonight), a spread too wide to time a pick on.
- Five Implosion calls on the imp estimate. Three (21:10:52–57) were Inner
  Demons imps (exactly 12.0 s apart) plus imps idling after an Implosion.
- Two Dreadstalkers-readiness calls, one shard reference disagreement, and
  one Ruination proc that expired 30 ms before the press.

The procs are deterministic: unused Infernal Bolt and Ruination procs ended
exactly 20.0 s after applying (16 of 16). The adapter now expires them,
which fixed the Ruination decision and changed no other in eight rescored
sessions.

Live, the shard hold cut Shadow Bolt's stale-reading icon flips from 34 of 95
casts to 5 of 98. Instant Demonbolts still flipped in 14 of 127, since their
+2 also lands after the success event on the client, so instants are now held
from the reading at UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT. No graded decision changes, because
graded presses rarely land in that 10–30 ms gap.
