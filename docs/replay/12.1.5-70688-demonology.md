# Demonology live review: 12.1.5 (70688), Nov. 1, 2026

The first two live Demonology sessions: a dummy pack and a full Timewalking
dungeon. Kairos agreed with the APL clearly more often than Assisted Combat in
both. Grading the icon on screen at each press against the APL rebuilt from the
combat log:

| Session | Kairos in game | Assisted Combat | Your casts |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Dummy pack (2.5 min) | **91/103 (88%)** | 77/103 (75%) | 96/109 (88%) |
| The Everbloom, Timewalking (10 min) | **177/222 (80%)** | 145/225 (64%) | 194/246 (79%) |

In the dungeon, Implosion accounts for 29 of Kairos's 45 misses. Outside it,
Kairos agrees on 164/180 (91%). The cause is imps the estimate can't see (see
below). The sessions found four live-model errors, now fixed, and one recorder
sampling flaw, which also affects Arcane.

## Inputs and coverage

- Character: Handrlock-Mal'Ganis, Demonology Diabolist (Diabolic Ritual, Reign
  of Tyranny, To Hell and Back, Secrets of the Coven, Ruination, Demoniac), about
  29% haste.
- Dummy: Cleave Training Dummy pack, open world, 10:38:05–10:40:32 Eastern.
  This ran the working tree after 0.2.1 with the Imp Lord cooldown fix.
- Dungeon: The Everbloom (Timewalking), 10:59–11:09, four boss kills. This added
  the Dreadstalkers, imp-lifetime and Implosion fixes plus keypress snapshots.
- One combat log, `WoWCombatLog-110126_102021.txt`. SavedVariables were frozen
  after each session under `translator/.local/replay/demo-20261101-dummy/` and
  `demo-20261101-everbloom/`.
- The dummy session had 109 decisions, 103 of them graded. The dungeon had 247,
  225 graded. Ungraded decisions had no aligned recorder cast or were out of
  combat.

## What the client exposes in combat

- Soul Shards stay readable (secrecy 0).
- The Demonic Core aura is secret in combat even in the open world, so the
  adapter uses Demonbolt's glow for presence only. A hidden Core count never
  changed a pick (0 of 355). For this build, only Power Siphon reads the count,
  and it isn't talented.
- After Grimoire: Imp Lord is cast, its spellbook entry resolves to 132411.

## Reference validity

The log never states shards: 12.1.5 cast events carry only mana. The replay
rebuilds shards from energize events (whole shards, despite a logged maximum of
50) and costs. At each client combat start, it reseeds from the client's last
traced reading, because shards regenerate between pulls. The model then matches
**94%** of later client readings within 0.15 s in both sessions (82/87 and
188/201).

Corrections the reference needed:

- An instant Demonbolt's +2 energize (ID 280127) is logged about 1 ms before
  its own CAST_SUCCESS. It is an effect of the press, so it is excluded from
  that press's state (26 and 86 presses).
- Costs from the client's readings: Hand of Gul'dan 3, Imp Lord 1, Tyrant 0,
  Ruination 0, Call Dreadstalkers 0.
- An imp's life ends at its own last action, not when its final Firebolt lands
  about a second later.

## Live-model errors found and fixed

| Error | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Imp Lord stayed recommended after casting | The resync that runs out of combat read the replaced spell as idle, in the same instant as the cast | An idle read can't cancel the cast's own cooldown within 2 s or while the spell is replaced |
| Dreadstalkers required and spent 2 shards | 7/7 casts free in client readings | No shard requirement or cost; APL regenerated |
| Imps expired after 5 Firebolts | Dummy: 59/87 Hand imps cast 6. Dungeon: 131/210 cast 6. None cast more | Lifetime is 6 Firebolts (12 s ÷ haste) |
| Implosion left no imps | 8/8 and 20/20 Implosions summoned 3 imps | With To Hell and Back, Implosion spawns 3 (talent link inferred) |
| Imps expired between pulls | Dungeon imps stopped casting for exactly the 18 s the player was out of combat, then resumed | The lifetime clock pauses out of combat |

## Wild Imps: the remaining gap

With all fixes, the replay's imp estimate gives the same APL pick as true imps
at 109/109 dummy decisions and 223/246 dungeon decisions. The remaining dungeon
misses are Implosion calls where the estimate is several imps short. Two causes
remain that Kairos can't see live:

- In combat, imps without a target stop casting. Six-cast imps lived a median
  10.2 s, but the 90th percentile was 16.2 s, against a modeled 9.3 s.
- Inner Demons adds an imp every 12.0 s. Its timer runs out of combat and shifted
  once (a 16.5 s gap), so its phase isn't visible. Counting one Inner Demons
  imp throughout combat made the estimate exact more often (5% → 22% of
  decisions) but changed no pick, so it isn't modeled.

Implosion is now on the spell watch, to test whether any visible field on it
tracks imps.

## Sampling: grade what was on screen at the press

The client starts the GCD at the keypress, and the 0.1 s display refresh often
evaluates the next GCD before UNIT_SPELLCAST_SENT, START or SUCCEEDED arrive.
Snapshots taken at those events had already advanced past the press:

- Dummy: 79 of 103 START/success snapshots.
- Dungeon: 119 of 225 snapshots, even at SENT.

For these sessions, the scorer replaces each such snapshot with the display
change shown just before that GCD began (its projected end minus one GCD).
Without that, the dummy scored 86/103 and the dungeon 168/224.

Going forward, the recorder keeps the last 20 evaluations by reference. At each
commit it reads the GCD start and saves the latest evaluation from before it,
with the later pick kept as `forecast`. Arcane's commitments get the same
correction. Arcane's scorer doesn't yet use SENT snapshots or the pick-stream
fallback.

## Remaining misses (dungeon, outside Implosion: 16)

These are mostly shard or proc timing at the press, plus Dreadstalkers coming
ready within the projected GCD. Rerunning the current adapter on the dungeon's
captured inputs agrees with the APL 173/224 times, against the 168/224 of those
same snapshots as captured. That run uses the old captured imp counts, so it
doesn't show the imp fixes.
